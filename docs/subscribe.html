Get Keep in Touch banners on your iPhone

Three quick steps. You only do this once.

This page needs your channel name. Open localhost:8787 on your Mac and scan the QR code there with your iPhone camera.

1Get the free ntfy app

Open the App Store

Tap Get. Already have it? Skip to step 2.

2Copy your channel name

It's private. Anyone with it could see your banners, so don't share it.

3Subscribe in ntfy

  1. Open ntfy.
  2. Tap + in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap the Topic name box, then tap it again and choose Paste.
  4. Leave Use another server off. Tap Subscribe.
  5. When it asks about notifications, tap Allow.

Then go back to your Mac and click Send test banner. A banner should pop up here within a few seconds.
